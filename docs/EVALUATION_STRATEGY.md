## Product narrative
“CtrlZ gets software back on track, and gives you the evidence to trust the recovery.”
The differentiator is the controlled recovery system around the model: external monitoring,
adaptive investigation, strategy comparison, contracts, safe execution and independent verification.
CampusPass demonstrates that system; it is not the main product.

## Presentation discipline
Lead with a customer's broken experience. Trigger a real fault. Let monitoring detect it.
Show what the agents observed, why an option was blocked, what executed, and the acceptance
checks. Close with the measured outcome and remaining risks. No fabricated savings, model
confidence percentages, synthetic timelines, or claimed production maturity.
